A service built around individual conversations

Thy & I is a university-admissions advising service I co-founded. The program I helped design was structured around three personalized 90-minute sessions, giving the advising a defined structure while keeping each student’s circumstances at its center.

The official service describes support for self-reflection and goals, extracurricular activities, application essays and scholarships, and a student community. The website is the entry point to that support.

From the service idea to a working website

I built the website and the web infrastructure for booking and payment. That work connected the public-facing explanation of the service with the practical steps needed to arrange and pay for an advising session.

My contribution covered both the product’s presentation and its supporting systems. The website was part of how the service operated, alongside the advising itself.

Designing the program with the team

As a co-founder, I also helped design the advising program. Working on the service and the software together meant thinking about the structure of the sessions and how the booking and payment systems would support it.

Connecting software with a real service

Thy & I gave me experience building the operational side of a people-centered service. It connects my interest in education with the practical work of turning a concept into a website and a usable workflow.
